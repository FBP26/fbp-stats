FAQ

Pool rules, pick entry, live probabilities, and how to get around the archive.

How do I make my picks?

Open Enter Picks, choose one team in every matchup, select one Best Bet, enter a week name and tiebreaker, then submit. Favorites are on the left, underdogs are on the right, and the NFL-designated home team is capitalized even at a neutral site.

Spreads are normally posted Tuesday and are locked once posted. You can replace a submitted card by submitting again with the same player name; the newest card becomes active.

How does the point spread work?

The spread handicaps the favorite. A favorite must win by more than the posted spread to cover. The underdog is correct if it wins outright or loses by fewer points than the spread.

If the favorite wins by exactly the spread, the game is a push. For example, with a 10-point favorite: a 30-14 score covers, 30-20 pushes, and 30-24 makes the underdog the correct pick.

What is the Best Bet?

Your Best Bet is an extra result attached to the team you trust most. A correct Best Bet adds one win and an incorrect Best Bet adds one loss. Under the pool's existing rules, a Best Bet push also counts as one additional loss.

How does the weekly tiebreaker work?

If players finish with the same number of wins, the closest guess to the combined net passing yards in the final game wins. Net passing yards include both teams' passing yards minus yards lost to sacks. Players equally close split the winnings.

When are picks due?

Submit before the first game of the week to be eligible under the normal deadline. The submission time is recorded. A late entry after the Thursday game may still play, but takes a loss for that game under the existing pool rule.

How do I read Current Week?

The main grid shows every active card, live pick results, record, rank, tiebreak position, paths to victory, and estimated win probability. Pending games remain unresolved; live games use the current against-the-spread leader provisionally.

The race playback shows how positions changed at recorded checkpoints. Scenario views explain which remaining outcomes help each player. Probabilities are estimates, not betting odds, and become exact only when all outcomes and the tiebreak are settled.

How do filters, charts, and tooltips work?

Use the selectors above a chart or table to change season, player, team, metric, or lookback. Click or tap a chart point, bar, or interactive table value for the exact sample and record. Click or tap it again, click outside, use the close button, or press Escape to dismiss it.

Pay attention to sample sizes. A strong percentage over a few picks is descriptive, not proof of a lasting tendency. Tables can be sorted by selecting their column headings.

What can I find in each history section?
  • Weeks: every card, weekly standings, and pick-by-pick separation.
  • Seasons: standings, weekly form, money, consistency, and the season race.
  • Players: records, streaks, team habits, and situational ATS splits.
  • Names: the complete week-name archive and recurring themes.
  • Teams: pool support, ATS outcomes, opponents, Best Bets, venues, and player affinity.
  • Games: one matchup with every archived pick and result.
How do the playoffs work?

The playoff winner has the most wins across all four rounds. Round 1 has 6 games plus a Best Bet for 7 possible wins; Round 2 has 4 games plus a Best Bet for 5; Round 3 has 2 games plus a Best Bet for 3; and the Super Bowl pick is worth 2 wins. That is 13 games and 17 possible wins.

The Super Bowl's combined net passing yards break a tie. Starting in Round 2, picks remain hidden until every player who can still win has submitted for that round.

What notifications are available?

The browser alert announces a newly opened regular-season week while FBP is open. Email preferences can cover a new slate, a move into first place, early and late game windows, Sunday and Monday night scenarios, and the final weekly result. An email address is inactive until its verification link is opened, and every message includes a one-click stop link.

Text alerts require a separate SMS provider and are not available yet. FBP does not save a phone number while text delivery is unavailable.

What visitor information is collected?

When anonymous analytics are enabled, FBP records page views, pick starts, submissions, a random browser/session identifier, referring domain, device class, viewport, language, and time zone. It does not collect IP addresses, precise location, names, picks, or full referring URLs for analytics.

The analytics are stored privately for site maintenance and are not shown on a public leaderboard or visitor page.
